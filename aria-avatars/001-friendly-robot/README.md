# 001 — Friendly Robot

**Palette:** Deep teal background (#0d1e26), cyan accent (#4ecdc4)
**Shape language:** Rounded rectangle head, soft ear panels, antenna with glowing tip
**Personality:** Warm, approachable, clearly a robot but non-threatening. The smile and expressive eyes push this toward assistant-brained rather than tool-brained.

## Interactions
- Hover: pupils shift (looking at you), antenna brightens, smile widens
- Works clearly at 16px — two cyan dots read as eyes even at tiny sizes

## Use case
Best for chat interfaces, notification badges, and anywhere Aria needs to feel inviting rather than technical.
